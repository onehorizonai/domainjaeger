# Code of Conduct

domainjaeger follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
It applies to everyone who takes part in this project: in issues, pull
requests, reviews and any other place where the project is discussed.

## In short

- Be kind and patient. Assume the other person means well.
- Talk about the work, not about the person.
- Accept that people differ in experience, language and background.
- No harassment, insults, sexual language or imagery, or publishing of
  someone's private information.

The full text of the Contributor Covenant is the rule. This summary is a
reminder.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour worries you, tell a maintainer in private. Use
GitHub's [report content](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
tools, or contact One Horizon, the organisation that maintains this project,
through its GitHub page:
[github.com/onehorizonai](https://github.com/onehorizonai). Please do not open
a public issue for this.

Reports are read and handled with discretion. The maintainers may remove,
edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that go
against this Code of Conduct, and may ban a person from the project when that
is what it takes.
